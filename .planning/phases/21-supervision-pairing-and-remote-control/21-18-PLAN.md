pending/stopping state, availability, and `safe_target_title` only. The title is
an at-most-80-character display label produced by the browser's sanitized tab
label policy with control/bidi stripping, URL/path/query/fragment and authority

`StopSupervisionControl`, `EndSelectedTabSupervision`, and

runtime requires exactly zero arguments for all three and re-resolves the exact
selected tab and service generation for every call.

snapshots or inventory-free health.

|---|---|---|

| T-22-06 | mitigate | Prefs and external health omit names, full IDs, permissions, content, paths, and authority; recursive sentinel tests gate every serializer. |
| T-22-08 | mitigate | The service persists IDs rather than pointers, re-queries after native lifecycle changes, invalidates generations before notification, and cancels late callbacks. |
| T-22-10 | mitigate | Fixed entry/string/reason/recovery/snapshot bounds have deterministic at-limit and over-limit tests and pruning. |
